FAQ

How much will my website actually cost?

We know budgets are a big deal. That's why we provide a clear breakdown of costs before we start—no hidden fees, no surprises. Whether it's a small business site or a complex platform, we'll tailor the project to fit your needs and budget.

All pricing is transparent and discussed upfront during our discovery call.

How long will it take to complete my website?

Timeline varies based on the complexity and scope of your project. Here's what you can typically expect:

  • Landing Pages: 1-2 weeks
  • 3-Page Websites: 2-3 weeks
  • 5-Page Business Websites: 3-4 weeks
  • 7+ Page Professional Sites: 5-7 weeks

We work efficiently without compromising quality. Your timeline starts after we receive your deposit and all necessary content. If you need your website faster, we offer express delivery options for an additional fee.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Every website we build at Nialure is fully mobile-responsive and optimized for all devices—smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

We follow a mobile-first design approach, which means we prioritize how your site looks and performs on mobile devices first, then enhance it for larger screens. With over 60% of web traffic coming from mobile, this ensures your visitors get a perfect experience no matter how they access your site.

We test rigorously on multiple devices and browsers before launch to guarantee everything works flawlessly.

What do I need to provide to get started?

To get your project started, we’ll need your website content (or let us know if you’d like copywriting support), your logo and brand assets, any images or graphics, your domain and hosting details (we can assist with setup if needed), and 2–3 website examples you like for inspiration. Once the contract is signed, we’ll send a clear onboarding checklist outlining everything required and the timelines.

Do you offer support after the website launches?

Yes, we're with you for the long run! At Nialure, we don't just build your website and vanish.

After launch, you receive 30 days of complimentary support to fix any bugs or technical issues directly related to our work. Beyond that, we offer flexible monthly maintenance packages.

We're committed to building long-term partnerships. Your success is our success, and we're always here to help your website grow with your business.
